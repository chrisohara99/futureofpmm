## Content Differentiation Statement

This SAP training curriculum is **substantively distinct** from the public Academy curriculum. The differentiation is documented in detail in `CURRICULUM-DIFFERENTIATION.md` and includes:

| Aspect | SAP Internal | Public Academy |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Unit Count** | 8 units | 12 units |
| **Format** | Workshop-based, scenario-driven | Reading-based, assessment-driven |
| **Voice** | Internal SAP perspective ("we") | Direct practitioner address ("you") |
| **Frameworks** | 10+ unique frameworks (Maturity Ladder, Visibility Stack, etc.) | Different framework set (10x PMM, AI Impact Matrix, etc.) |
| **Examples** | SAP-specific scenarios and tools | Vendor-neutral, industry-agnostic |
| **Exercises** | Discussion prompts, team activities | Individual quizzes and assessments |

The two curricula share a **common subject matter** (product marketing in the AI era) and **common authorship** (Chris O'Hara), but are distinct works with different pedagogical approaches, structures, and content.
