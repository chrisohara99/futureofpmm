# 📚 UNIT 7 UNLOCKING THIS WEEK

## The Demo Stack — Product Storytelling Reimagined

### Core Thesis
> "The most powerful demo element isn't your product. It's proof that someone else solved the same problem."

### What's Covered

**The Demo Maturity Model**
| Level | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Feature Tour | "Let me show you what our product does" |
| 2 | Use Case Demo | "Let me show you how you'd solve X" |
| 3 | Customer Story | "Let me show you how [customer] solved Y" |
| 4 | Co-Created Discovery | "Let's explore your problem together" |

**Target:** Most demos should be Level 3-4. Level 1 should be rare.

### The Demo Architecture
1. **Opening** (5-10 min) — Establish you understand their problem
2. **Problem Exploration** (10-15 min) — Make the pain vivid
3. **Vision** (5-10 min) — Paint life after
4. **Proof** (15-20 min) — Product + reference
5. **Bridge** (5-10 min) — Connect to next steps

### Workshop Exercise
- Demo Audit: Rate your last 3 demos on the maturity model
- Discussion: What would it take to move up one level?
