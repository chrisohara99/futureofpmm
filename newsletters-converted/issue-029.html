Unit 11: Skills That Survive

The question we hear most often—at conferences, in DMs, over drinks at industry dinners—is some version of: "Should I be worried about my job?"

Here's the honest answer: Your job isn't going away. But the version of it you're doing today probably is.

The Four Surviving Skills

🧠 Strategic Judgment — Decisions under ambiguity

🤝 Organizational Intelligence — Navigating stakeholders ✍️ Narrative Craft — Stories that move people 💜 Customer Empathy — Understanding what customers feel

The PMM of 2023 spent most of their time on execution—producing deliverables. The PMM of 2028 will spend most of their time on judgment—making decisions that agents can't make. Unit 11 shows you how to develop each skill deliberately.

👤 Thought Leader Series

John Koblinsky: The Missing Layer in AI Research

John Koblinsky is Director of Research and Insights at SAP Concur—where he's spent years figuring out how to study what thousands of organizations actually do with their procurement and travel data. When AI-generated research started proliferating, he developed the Evidence Assurance framework.

"Evidence Assurance is simply the discipline of knowing where claims come from, how reliable those sources are, and what confidence level they warrant. AI makes this more important, not less—because now we can produce authoritative-sounding content at scale without any underlying authority."

The framework distinguishes between four evidence levels, from primary research with named sources down to AI-generated synthesis that requires human verification. Essential reading for anyone producing research-backed content.
