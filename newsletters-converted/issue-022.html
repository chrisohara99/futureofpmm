📚 This Week's Focus: Unit 6

Content, Thought Leadership & the Narrative Advantage

Awareness Stage · 45 minutes

Why does 80% of B2B content fail to generate pipeline? And how does AI change both the problem and the solution?

Unit 6 tackles the content collapse — the phenomenon where more content hasn't meant more impact. The solution isn't just "better content." It's understanding that the audience has changed: AI agents now consume, summarize, and recommend content on behalf of human buyers.

You'll learn:

  • The 80/20 Pattern: Why most content gets ignored—and what the top 20% does differently
  • Performative vs. Genuine thought leadership: How to tell the difference (and why it matters for AI)
  • GEO: The Agent as Reader: How AI consumes content differently than humans
  • The Three-Layer Content Operating Model for AI-augmented production
