The Demo Stack — Product Storytelling Reimagined

Unit 7 is live: The demo that won a $12M deal against Snowflake and Databricks—and what it teaches us about product storytelling. The Demo Maturity Model (Levels 1-4), vendor updates on Consensus, Demostack & Storylane, plus 42% of B2B buyers now start research in AI tools.

This week we unlock Unit 7: The Demo Stack — and I want to start with a story.

Last year, one of our teams competed for a $12M deal against Snowflake and Databricks. Scorecards were tied. The decision came down to the executive presentation.

Snowflake showed 60 minutes of features. Standard fare.

Our team started with the customer's problem. They made the pain vivid. Then they showed how it would work with SAP. The actual product demo? Maybe 15 minutes. The rest was story.

"It was the only presentation that felt like it was about us. Everyone else was showing their product. You showed our problem."*

— The CIO, afterward

We won.

That's what Unit 7 is about: the demo stack redefined — not product demonstration, but problem storytelling with product proof.

—Chris

Level

Name The Approach 1 Feature Tour "Let me show you what our product does" 2 Use Case Demo "Let me show you how you'd solve X" 3 Customer Story "Let me show you how [customer] solved Y" 4 Co-Created Discovery "Let's explore your problem together"

Target: Most demos should be Level 3-4. Level 1 should be rare.
