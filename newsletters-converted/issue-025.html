The Curriculum Continues: Segmentation & Sizing

The curriculum is no longer 8 units. It's 9—and growing.

This week we're releasing Unit 9: Segmentation & Sizing. Before you can position a product, enable a sales team, or run a campaign, you need to answer a deceptively simple question: who are we going after?

This unit covers market segmentation (category, competition, growth quadrants), building TAM with AI assistance, customer segmentation (funnel stages, buying roles), and buyer tiers from SMB to Enterprise.
