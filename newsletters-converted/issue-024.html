📰 News Roundup

The Intelligence Layer Fight

The must-read this week is from DMNews: "Marketing automation platforms spent the first half of 2026 compressing the time from insight to campaign launch from days to minutes—and the fight nobody is naming yet is over who owns that layer of intelligence."

The key insight: Most agentic AI platforms are running on the same OpenAI or Anthropic models underneath. The interface is different. The workflow branding is different. But the reasoning engine is frequently the same one your competitor is renting.

If the intelligence itself is a shared utility, what's left to compete on? The data it's trained on. The decision logic. The memory of what actually worked. That's the layer that matters—and that's exactly what Unit 8 explores from a pricing perspective.
