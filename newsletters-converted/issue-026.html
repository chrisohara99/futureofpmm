Unit 10: Buyer Personas & Journey Mapping

The curriculum keeps growing. Unit 10 is now live.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: most B2B personas are fiction. They're created in workshops by people who've never spoken to a customer, then pinned to walls and promptly ignored.

AI changes this. New tools build personas from behavioral signals — what buyers actually do, not what they say. And with buying groups now averaging 4-10 people, you can't afford to map just one decision-maker.
