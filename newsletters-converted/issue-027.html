Units 9 & 10 Are Now Available

Two new units have gone live this week, and together they address what might be the most persistent gap in how product marketers think about their audiences — the space between the market we imagine and the buyers who actually show up.

📊 Unit 9: Segmentation & Sizing

Segmentation has always been the foundation of go-to-market strategy, but AI-powered approaches are changing what's possible — moving beyond demographic proxies toward behavioral signals that reveal how buyers actually make decisions, and sizing methodologies that can hold up when the CFO asks hard questions.

🎯 Unit 10: Buyer Personas & Journey Mapping

The traditional persona — assembled in a workshop from assumptions and intuition — has never been particularly reliable, and in an era of AI-assisted research it's worth asking whether we can do better. This unit explores persona development grounded in observable behavior, not hypothetical preferences.

📰 AI + PMM News

What's Moving in AI + PMM

Gartner on Agentic AI Adoption

The latest forecast suggests 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI capabilities by 2028, up from less than 1% in 2024 — a pace that, if it holds, would mean 15% of day-to-day work decisions being made autonomously.

AI as Shopping Discovery Channel

A recent survey found 72% of holiday shoppers consulting AI for gift ideas, which suggests ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini have crossed into mainstream consumer behavior faster than many expected.

The Martech Integration Gap

Gartner reports 78% of CMOs say their martech stack isn't performing as expected — a reminder that AI-powered tools tend to expose, rather than fix, underlying data quality problems.

Agentic AI Gets Its Own Award Category

The MarTech Breakthrough Awards created an "Agentic AI Platform of the Year" category this year — one of those small signals that suggests the term has moved from hype cycle to established taxonomy.
