⭐ User of the Week

Sean Thomson: Gartner's Six Signals for PMM

Sean Thomson, Product Marketing Strategist at SAP, attended the Gartner Marketing Symposium and came back with a coherent synthesis instead of a pile of slide photos. The through-line: PMM is evolving from messaging and launches into strategic GTM orchestration.

Signal 1: AI Transforms the Operating Model

PMMs design and govern AI workflows, not just use AI tools.

Signal 2: Market Intel Goes Strategic

Competitive advantage shifts to insight generation and signal detection.

Signal 3: PMM as GTM Orchestrator

Aligning Product, Marketing, Sales, CS around unified GTM strategy.

Signal 4: Customer Growth in Scope

Adoption, retention, expansion increasingly showing up in PMM OKRs.

Signal 5: COE Model Returns

Centralized standards + federated execution for AI-generated content.

Signal 6: Market Shaping, Not Just Execution

Successful CMOs shape customer preferences, not just deliver results.

"AI won't replace PMM. It will elevate PMM into a more strategic role—if we're willing to evolve with it." — Sean Thomson
