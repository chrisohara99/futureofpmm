📣 Important Update

Thank You — And What's Next

When we launched The Future of PMM earlier this year, we weren't sure what to expect. Would people show up? Would the content resonate? Would anyone actually complete the curriculum?

The answer, it turns out, was yes — in ways that exceeded our expectations. A small cohort of test users dove in, provided invaluable feedback, stress-tested the labs, and pushed through all twelve units of material. You helped us prove something important: that PMMs are hungry for practical guidance on navigating the AI transformation, and that a structured curriculum — not just scattered blog posts — is the right way to deliver it.

🚀 The Site Is Moving to SAP Corporate Infrastructure

Based on the success of this pilot, we're migrating The Future of PMM to SAP's corporate servers — where it can scale to serve all SAP PMMs and integrate with the tools you use every day. The current site at futureofpmm.com will be sunset in the coming weeks.

What this means for you: Consume as much of the current site as you can while it's still live. Complete the curriculum, explore the labs, bookmark the articles. The content will carry forward to the new platform, but there may be a transition period — and the best way to ensure you don't miss anything is to finish it now.

What's coming: A corporate-approved platform that will help all SAP PMMs advance their work and build the Book of Work. Same mission, bigger reach, better integration. Stay tuned for announcements.

🎓 Earn Your Certificate

Complete the Curriculum, Get Certified

All twelve units are now live — from foundational concepts through the capstone assessment. Complete them all with passing scores, and you'll earn a certificate that recognizes your investment in AI-era product marketing skills.

The curriculum covers: AI transformation fundamentals, competitive intelligence, messaging strategy, content for two audiences (humans + AI), segmentation, buyer personas, GEO visibility, the skills that survive automation, and more.

This is your chance to finish before the migration. Don't leave units incomplete — the certificate carries forward, but only if you earn it first.
