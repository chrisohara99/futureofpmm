From 80+ Daily Digests — What Matters Most

We publish a daily AI + PMM digest every morning. These are the stories with the most impact on how we work:

At TDX, Salesforce exposed its entire platform as APIs, MCP tools, and CLI commands for AI agents to operate without browsers. Enterprise CRM is being rebuilt agent-first.

Adobe is replacing its legacy platform with an agentic system built around "Coworkers" — persistent AI agents with enterprise memory that orchestrate marketing autonomously.

Oracle is cutting thousands of jobs as it restructures around AI and cloud. A bellwether for enterprise software: efficiency gains from AI are translating into headcount reductions.

HubSpot's new model ties pricing to business outcomes, not seats or features. A preview of how AI changes software economics — pay for results, not access.

SAP's acquisition of the master data management leader signals a push to own the enterprise data foundation layer. Clean data is the fuel for AI — and SAP wants to control it.
