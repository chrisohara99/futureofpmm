### This Week: Unit 7 Unlocks — The Demo Stack

---

**The Lead**

Last year, one of our teams competed for a $12M deal against Snowflake and Databricks. The technical evaluators had run bake-offs. The scorecards were tied. The decision came down to the executive presentation.

Snowflake showed a polished product demo—60 minutes of features, capabilities, competitive comparisons. Standard fare.

Our team did something different. They started with the customer's problem—a specific scenario the CIO had described in an earlier meeting. They showed how that scenario played out today (painful). Then they showed how it would work with SAP (seamless). They brought a customer reference who had faced the same challenge.

The product demo was maybe 15 minutes. The rest was story.

We won the deal.

*"It was the only presentation that felt like it was about us,"* the CIO said later. *"Everyone else was showing their product. You showed our problem."*

This is what Unit 7 is about: **the demo stack redefined—not product demonstration, but problem storytelling with product proof.**

### 📚 Unit 7 Preview: The Demo Maturity Model

The core framework you'll learn this week:

| Level | Name | The Approach |
|-------|------|--------------|
| **1** | Feature Tour | "Let me show you what our product does" |
| **2** | Use Case Demo | "Let me show you how you'd solve [scenario]" |
| **3** | Customer Story | "Let me show you how [customer] solved [problem]" |
| **4** | Co-Created Discovery | "Let's explore your problem together" |

**The target:** Most demos should be Level 3-4. Level 1 should be rare.

**Workshop this week:** Audit your last three demos. What level were they? What would it take to move up one level?
