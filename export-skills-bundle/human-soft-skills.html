The Core Insight

Humans guide and decide what matters. AI produces content.
It's always a plus+ — not AI or human.

How We Collaborate

  • Produces content

  • Synthesize
  • Judge

  • Think systemically
  • Ask better questions
  • Verify
  • Create meaning

  • Understand stakeholders

Thinking

10 skills
Skill What it's about What AI does Why humans matter more
Stakeholder ManagementAddress audiences correctly with right arguments, empathyCan tailor styles and simulate viewpoints, but doesn't know your specific stakeholders, relationships, or contextHumans know what matters to stakeholders, anticipate reactions, build trust, customize output
Systems ThinkingUnderstand how things connect and influence each otherOften optimizes one task but misses broader context and downstream effectsHumans understand the whole system, anticipate impacts, avoid unintended consequences
SynthesisCombine ideas from many sources into something meaningfulCreates many ideas but delivers fragments; information becomes abundant but siloedHumans combine sources meaningfully into coherent decisions and narratives
Decision MakingSteer AI direction. AI gives options, humans chooseGenerates options, recommendations, scenarios. But AI needs directionHumans decide direction, when enough info exists, which path to pursue
Judgement & Trade-offsDecide what's relevant, what creates valueCan generate options but struggles when priorities conflictGood judgement decides what matters. Evaluate trade-offs: speed vs quality, cost vs risk
Fact CheckingKnow what to verify and howDoes summaries but may misinterpret, hallucinate, or fabricateVerification is core. Look through output, check sources, verify numbers
Critical ThinkingDetect AI hallucinations, errors, biasesCan be very convincing while wrong — may lack context or introduce biasActively challenge conclusions, evaluate evidence, identify logical gaps
Embed PoliciesKnow relevant company, state, regulatory policiesCan follow when instructed but doesn't know which applyEnsure compliance — know rules, company policies, apply correctly
AI Ethics & PrivacyEnsure work is free from bias, respects privacyLearns from potentially biased data; doesn't know what's in trainingHandle input carefully, review for bias, protect sensitive information
Metacognition ⭐The crown jewel: think about your own thinkingCan reinforce existing beliefs confidently — agreement ≠ correctnessReflect on assumptions, don't anchor on first answer. Self-awareness is key

Collaborate with AI

5 skills
Skill What it's about What AI does Why humans matter more
Speed Reading / Info TriageIdentify important info fastGenerates massive amounts in secondsQuickly identify relevance, distinguish signal from noise, set focus
Context BuildingGive AI right background — enough, not too muchUses context but assumes when missingDetermine relevant info. Too little = wrong assumptions. Too much = distraction
Problem FramingDefine the right problem (AI solves beautifully — even wrong ones)Generates for the problem given, can't reflect if it's rightDefining right problem is now critical — distinguish symptoms from root causes
Asking Good QuestionsGood, neutral questions maximize output qualityOutput quality = question quality. Vague = off-targetValue shifts to better questions. Break into objectives, constraints, risks
Editing & RefinementRefine prompts and results iterativelyFirst output rarely final. Refinement improves qualityEvaluate results, spot weaknesses, improve structure/wording/logic

Mindset

9 skills
Skill What it's about What AI does Why humans matter more
Refinement LoopsIterative mindset: prompt → evaluate → adjust → repeatIncorporates feedback, iteratesQuality from multiple cycles. Learn from each iteration
AI CultureSupport team culture of how/when to use AIMakes work efficient if humans embrace itFoster positivity, help others, find your balance
Sustainability AwarenessEnvironmental impact of AI usageRuns on data centers, energy, infrastructureNot every task needs AI. Balance value with responsible use
Communication about AIBe positive representative of AI cultureCreates excitement but also concernsHow you talk influences perception. Advocate while noting where oversight matters
Human CreativityKeep creativity! It sets you apartGenerates by combining patterns — inspires but doesn't truly createHumans use experience, emotion, intuition to create true new things
Intellectual HumilityWillingness to be wrong — AI agreeing ≠ you're rightConfidently agrees (sycophancy)Stay open to being wrong. Ask AI to challenge assumptions
CuriosityWillingness to experiment with AISupports experiments, may surprise youTry new things, discover capabilities. Ask: "How could AI help?"
Cognitive IndependenceFind your human/AI balanceCan perform many cognitive tasksAvoid outsourcing all thinking. Know when judgment should overrule
PatienceAI collaboration is a new skill needing trainingProvides instant answersLearning takes time. Persist through experimentation
