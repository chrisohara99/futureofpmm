## Executive Summary

The SAP 8-unit curriculum has been **completely rewritten** to be substantively distinct from the public 12-unit curriculum. While both address product marketing in the AI era, they use:

- Different structural approaches
- Different conceptual frameworks
- Different pedagogical methods
- Different examples and scenarios
- Different voice and tone

## Detailed Differentiation

### Structural Differences

| Aspect | SAP (8-Unit) | Public (12-Unit) |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| Unit count | 8 | 12 |
| Format | Workshop scenarios + exercises | Lecture + assessment |
| Opening | Narrative scenarios | Statistics and research |
| Exercises | Integrated throughout | Quiz at end |
| Progression | Discussion-based | Content consumption |

### Framework Differences

**SAP uses these unique frameworks (not present in public curriculum):**

1. **The Maturity Ladder** (Unit 1)
   - Skeptic → Accelerator → Augmenter → Architect
   - Four stages of PMM AI adoption

2. **Three Buckets Model** (Unit 1)
   - Production / Translation / Judgment
   - Categorization of PMM work types

3. **The Visibility Stack** (Unit 2)
   - Documentation / Validation / Context / Consistency
   - Four layers determining AI discoverability

4. **The Intelligence Spectrum** (Unit 3)
   - Reactive → Periodic → Continuous → Predictive
   - Competitive intelligence maturity levels

5. **The Evidence Hierarchy** (Unit 4)
   - Tier 1: Verified third-party / Tier 2: Credible internal / Tier 3: Marketing assertions
   - Evidence credibility classification

6. **Knowledge Architecture** (Unit 5)
   - Six building blocks: Positioning, Proof Points, Objection Handlers, Competitive Intel, Technical Depth, Commercial Guidance
   - Modular enablement structure

7. **The Narrative Framework** (Unit 6)
   - World View → Implication → Path → Proof → Role
   - Five-layer narrative construction

8. **Content Tiers** (Unit 6)
   - Tier 1: Narrative / Tier 2: Proof / Tier 3: Distribution
   - Content strategy prioritization

9. **Demo Maturity Model** (Unit 7)
   - Feature Tour → Use Case → Customer Story → Co-Created Discovery
   - Demo effectiveness progression

10. **Demo Architecture** (Unit 7)
    - Opening → Problem Exploration → Vision → Proof → Bridge
    - Five-phase demo structure

**Public curriculum uses different frameworks:**
- 10x PMM concept
- AI Impact Matrix (with percentages)
- Different section structures throughout

### Voice Differences

**SAP (Internal):**
> "We've been running AI pilots across SAP's PMM organization for eighteen months. What emerged wasn't a simple 'AI replaces tasks' story—it was a maturity pattern."

**Public (Direct):**
> "The activities that consume most of your time are exactly where AI has the greatest impact. This isn't a threat—it's an opportunity."

### Example Differences

**SAP uses SAP-specific scenarios:**
- Marcus Chen and the midnight RFP
- SAP EMEA competitive loss analysis
- SAP Datasphere positioning examples
- SAP-specific sales enablement challenges

**Public uses vendor-neutral examples:**
- Generic PMM scenarios
- Multi-vendor references
- Industry-agnostic case studies

## Legal Differentiation Summary

These curricula are distinct works because:

1. **Different unit count and structure** (8 vs. 12)
2. **Different conceptual frameworks** (10+ unique SAP frameworks)
3. **Different pedagogical approach** (workshop vs. reading)
4. **Different voice and perspective** (internal "we" vs. external "you")
5. **Different examples and scenarios** (SAP-specific vs. generic)
6. **Different progression and exercises** (discussion-based vs. assessment-based)

The SAP curriculum is a derivative work that uses the underlying intellectual property of Chris O'Hara's book manuscript while presenting substantially different content, structure, and pedagogical approach.
