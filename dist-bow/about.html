🧪 A Living Experiment

The Future of PMM isn't a theoretical exercise. It's a real-time experiment in what happens when you give a team of product marketers at a $200B enterprise company the tools, frameworks, and permission to rebuild their craft for the AI era.

The hypothesis: Product marketing is uniquely positioned to benefit from AI — not through automation, but through augmentation. The skills that make great PMMs (synthesis, positioning, narrative) are amplified, not replaced, by AI tools.

The curriculum is based on The Future of Product Marketing, a book written in December 2025 that laid out the frameworks and predictions for how AI would transform the discipline. What started as a thesis has become a living laboratory — every unit, every lab, every assessment shaped by feedback from PMMs applying these concepts to actual products, actual competitors, and actual customers.

📚 What We're Testing

The curriculum covers the full spectrum of how AI changes product marketing work:

The Visibility Shift — How buyers discover products is changing. 42% of B2B buyers now start research in AI tools. We're learning how to position for both human readers and the AI systems that mediate discovery.

The Evaluator Layer — Between you and the buyer sits a new layer: AI agents and industry analysts. The skills that win one win the other. We're building frameworks for satisfying both.

The Tool Stack — From Copilot to Claude to Canva, we're documenting what actually works in enterprise PMM workflows. Not demos — real usage, real results, real governance considerations.

The Commercial Model — Pricing, packaging, and go-to-market all change when AI agents become buyers. We're exploring outcome-based models and the skills PMMs need to navigate them.
