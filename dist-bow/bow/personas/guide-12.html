The Four Surviving Skills

As you move forward, remember that technical fluency with AI tools is now table stakes. Your differentiator is one of these four capabilities:

  • Strategic Judgment — Making good decisions under ambiguity with incomplete information
  • Organizational Intelligence — Navigating stakeholders and building cross-functional alignment
  • Narrative Craft — Constructing arguments that move people through story, specificity, and voice
  • Customer Empathy — Understanding what customers feel, not just what they need

Which one is yours? That's the vertical bar of your T-shape—the skill that makes you irreplaceable.
