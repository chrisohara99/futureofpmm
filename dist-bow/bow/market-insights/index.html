PMM Leads · Area 03

Market Insights & Signal Synthesis

PMM systematically gathers and synthesizes buyer feedback — adoption blockers, persona priorities, pain points — and routes structured insights back to Product Management.

What PMM Owns

PMM owns insight synthesis and application to positioning, messaging, roadmap feedback, win/loss, and GTM. Research may be run by CMI, AR, Product, or other teams — but PMM synthesizes findings into actionable market intelligence.

Key responsibilities:

  • Synthesize customer, analyst, competitive, and field insights
  • Route structured intelligence to Product Management and GTM
  • Maintain competitive battlecards and win/loss analysis
  • Track AI visibility and GEO performance
  • Inform positioning and messaging evolution
