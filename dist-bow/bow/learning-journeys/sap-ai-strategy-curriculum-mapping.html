SAP's All-in on AI Strategy

How the Focus Programs connect to your PMM enablement — and why this curriculum matters now

By Chris O'Hara · August 2026

From Strategy to Reality

The Autonomous Enterprise is SAP's direction. The All-in on AI Focus Programs are how we get there — turning ambition into coordinated execution across every part of the company, at the speed the moment demands.

The goal is clear: become the #1 business AI company, powering the Autonomous Enterprise.

But transformation at this scale doesn't happen overnight. It happens through the work people do every day. The Focus Programs connect your contribution — whatever your role — to SAP's bigger story. They also create the space to build AI fluency: not as a one-time effort, but as part of how we grow.

"A signed contract only matters when it leads to adoption and measurable results."

This is the fundamental shift. Growth is driven by customers using AI, seeing real value, and expanding their usage over time — not just by closing deals.

Why This Matters for You

This curriculum isn't just professional development — it's directly aligned with how SAP is transforming. When you complete a unit, you're building skills that map to active Focus Programs with executive sponsorship, KPIs, and real business outcomes. Your growth as a PMM professional is part of SAP's growth as an AI company.

The Key Metrics

The Focus Programs are measured against three company-wide priorities:

  1. Increase EBT & FI Revenue — through differentiation and net new wedge
  2. Accelerate growth within active customer base — land and expand
  3. Drive operational efficiency — disciplined workflow transformation

Every unit in this curriculum helps you contribute to at least one of these outcomes — whether through better positioning, more effective enablement, smarter pricing models, or clearer customer journeys.
