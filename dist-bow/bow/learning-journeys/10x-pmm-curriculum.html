The 10x PMM Curriculum: What's Coming and Why

Learning Journeys

Every PMM team now has access to AI tools. Copilot, Claude, Perplexity, CompeteGPT — the stack is there. But access isn't leverage.

The gap isn't tools. It's behavior. Most PMMs are still using AI like a faster search engine: "write me a blog post," "summarize this call," "draft this email." The output is... fine. Generic. Forgettable.

Meanwhile, a small group of PMMs are doing something different. They're not outsourcing work to AI — they're co-working with it. And the difference is everything.

The Frame: Co-Work, Don't Outsource

This is the mental model that changes everything:

Outsourcing (wrong): "Write this for me."
First draft shipped. Skill atrophies. Output is generic.

Co-working (right): "Push back on my thinking."
Iterate like sparring partners. Skill compounds. Output is distinctive.

When you outsource to AI, you get commodity output. Anyone can do that. When you co-work with AI, you get leverage on your judgment — the one thing that's actually hard to replicate.

💡 The 10x PMM isn't doing 10x the output.

It's the one whose judgment is 10x harder to replicate. AI handles the drafts, research, rewrites, and QA. You reinvest that time in customer conversations, narrative, alignment, taste, and original POV.

The Structure: Three Phases

We're building a structured learning program with three distinct phases, each building on the last:

Phase 1

Foundations

Prompting fundamentals, tool stack, governance guardrails

Phase 2

Co-Working

Drafting with pushback, research synthesis, real deliverables

Phase 3

Building

Interactive artifacts, agent workflows, portfolio development

Phase 1: Foundations

Everyone starts at the same baseline. Prompting fundamentals — not the "tips and tricks" version, but the mental model for treating AI as a sparring partner. Tool stack orientation so nobody says "I didn't know we had that." Governance guardrails so everyone knows what's in bounds.

The bar: No one moves forward without working access, baseline fluency, and a documented routine.

Phase 2: Co-Working

This is where the behavior change happens. Drafting with pushback — the opposite of outsourcing. Research and competitive synthesis using multi-tool workflows. Function-specific modules tailored to how each team actually works.

The bar: Real work ships. Analyst responses, competitive teardowns, launch briefs — all co-worked with AI. Not sandbox exercises.

Phase 3: Building

From user to builder. Interactive artifacts — ROI calculators, dashboards, React components built with Claude Code. Agent workflows that compress recurring PMM tasks. Portfolio development and graduation.

The bar: Every PMM graduates with a portfolio, not a certificate. Evidence of co-working, not just attendance.

Function-Specific Tracks

One curriculum, multiple tracks. Each function gets modules tailored to how they actually work:

Six Functional Tracks

  • Commercialization: Pricing scenario engines, deal-desk drafting agents, bundle narrative generators
  • Technical PMM: Interactive ROI calculators, demo script generators, RFP automation
  • Outbound: Analyst response workflows, launch variant testing, field enablement at depth
  • BDC: Competitive teardowns, positioning frameworks, customer proof synthesis
  • Research: Competitive intel engines, analyst tracking automation, source synthesis playbooks
  • Field + Partner: Partner playbook generators, channel narrative workflows, industry-specific enablement

Every function lead has a unit that lands hardest for their team. That's the one to show up to, coach, and demo from.

AI Labs: Hands-On Practice

The curriculum includes interactive AI Labs — tools that take concepts from the units and turn them into actionable outputs for your actual work:

  • PMM Activity Audit — Map your activities to the AI Impact Matrix
  • GEO Visibility Test — Score your product's AI discoverability
  • CI Brief Generator — Generate deal-specific competitive intel
  • Agent Positioning Test — See your page through AI procurement eyes
  • Content Layer Audit — Classify content against the 20/40/40 model
  • Demo Narrative Builder — Transform features into transformation stories

Each lab takes real inputs and uses AI to generate tailored outputs you can use immediately.

Why This Matters Now

PMM output is being commoditized. Agents can draft briefs and decks. That's not a threat — it's an opportunity to reclaim time for the work that actually differentiates:

  • Customer conversations that surface insights no AI can find
  • Narrative that creates categories, not just describes them
  • Judgment calls on positioning, timing, and tone
  • Relationships with analysts, customers, and sales

The curriculum isn't about keeping up with AI. It's about getting ahead of the shift — so when agents handle 80% of production work, we're already spending that time on the 20% that matters.

"The goal isn't AI fluency. The goal is to build things we couldn't build 90 days ago. That's the whole game."
