Unit 9

Persona Development with AI

Opening Scenario: The Persona That Wasn't

Sarah, a PMM at SAP's CX unit, was presenting buyer personas to her sales enablement team when the VP of Sales interrupted: "These are the same three personas we've had for four years. Are enterprise CX buyers really still the same people making the same decisions the same way?"

She knew he was right. The "Digital Transformation Leader" persona had been created in 2021, before AI changed everything. The buying process, the stakeholders involved, the questions they asked—all had shifted. But updating personas the traditional way—months of research, dozens of interviews, agency fees—felt impossible with her current bandwidth.

Two weeks later, Sarah had rebuilt her persona framework using AI-assisted research. She analyzed 200+ Gong calls, synthesized G2 reviews, processed analyst reports, and validated findings through targeted interviews. The result: personas that reflected how buyers actually behave today, not how they behaved in 2021.

The question this unit explores: How do you build personas that stay current in a world where buyer behavior evolves faster than traditional research cycles?

The Persona Problem in the Agentic Era

Traditional persona development has three fundamental problems:

Problem 1: Time Lag

By the time traditional research completes, buyer behavior has shifted. The six-month persona project delivers insights that are already stale.

Problem 2: Sample Bias

Interview-based research talks to buyers willing to be interviewed—often not representative of your actual buyer population. You hear from the friendly ones, not the skeptical ones who matter most.

Problem 3: Static Artifacts

Personas become PowerPoint slides that live in forgotten folders. They're not integrated into workflows or updated as markets evolve.

Key Insight

AI doesn't replace the human judgment in persona development—it amplifies your ability to gather evidence and stay current. The strategic decisions about who to target and how to position remain irreducibly human.

The AI-Augmented Persona Framework

Modern persona development combines multiple data streams that AI can process at scale:

Data Source What AI Extracts Human Judgment Required
Sales Call Recordings (Gong/Chorus) Questions asked, objections raised, decision criteria mentioned Pattern significance, strategic implications
G2/TrustRadius Reviews Pain points, value language, competitive mentions Which segments to prioritize
LinkedIn Profiles Career paths, skill patterns, content engagement Relevance to buying influence
CRM Data Deal velocity, stakeholder involvement, loss reasons What drives wins vs. losses
Community Forums Questions, complaints, workarounds Unmet needs worth addressing

The Four-Layer Persona Model

Layer 1: Demographics & Firmographics

The traditional basics—title, company size, industry—but enriched with behavioral data. Not just "VP of IT" but "VP of IT who's been in role 18-36 months and is under pressure to show AI transformation progress."

Layer 2: Information Behavior

How do they research? What sources do they trust? Do they use AI assistants for vendor research? This layer determines your content strategy.

Layer 3: Decision Process

Who else is involved? What's the sequence? What kills deals? This layer shapes your sales enablement.

Layer 4: Language Patterns

What words do they use to describe their problems? What jargon signals credibility? This layer drives your messaging and content creation.

Agentic Shift

Buyers increasingly use AI assistants to research vendors. Your persona must now include: "How does this persona's AI assistant evaluate solutions?" Your content must be parseable by both the human and their AI.

Workshop Exercise: The Persona Refresh

Take one of your existing personas. Using AI tools, gather evidence from three sources:

  1. Gong/call recordings: What questions has this persona asked in the last 90 days?
  2. G2 reviews: What language do they use to describe value?
  3. LinkedIn: What's their typical career path? What content do they engage with?

Deliverable: A one-page persona update highlighting what's changed and what the implications are for your positioning.

From Persona to Action

A persona is only useful if it changes behavior. Each persona should connect to:

  • Messaging guidance: Specific language patterns and proof points
  • Content recommendations: What formats, topics, and depth levels resonate
  • Objection handling: Predictable concerns and effective responses
  • Competitive positioning: How this persona evaluates alternatives

The Living Persona System

Instead of static documents, build a persona system that:

  • Pulls from live data sources weekly
  • Flags when patterns shift significantly
  • Integrates directly with content creation workflows
  • Informs sales call prep in real-time

Key Principles (Summary)

  1. Evidence over intuition. AI enables you to ground personas in data from hundreds of interactions, not a handful of interviews.
  2. Continuous over periodic. Build systems that update personas as markets evolve, not once-a-year refreshes.
  3. Behavioral over demographic. Focus on how buyers behave, not just who they are on paper.
  4. Action-oriented over descriptive. Every persona element should connect to a specific PMM action.
  5. AI-aware. Modern personas must account for how buyers' AI assistants research and recommend solutions.

Before Unit 10

Assignment: Pick one persona and use Claude or ChatGPT to analyze 10+ G2 reviews from buyers who match that persona. Document:

  • What language do they use to describe their problems?
  • What value do they highlight?
  • What concerns do they raise?
  • How would this change your current messaging?
